Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect almost nothing. Here is exactly what happens when you visit Furlore.

What we collect

  • No analytics. We run no Google Analytics, Plausible, Matomo, or other trackers. No analytics cookies.
  • GitHub Pages logs HTTP requests (IP, user-agent, page) as part of standard hosting infrastructure. We do not access or store these logs.
  • localStorage for two preferences: theme (dark/light) and age-gate confirmation. These stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link you are taken via /go/dating/ to a third-party service.

  • We set referrerpolicy="no-referrer" on all affiliate links; the source page URL is not sent.
  • We pass short sub-ID slugs (page, language, placement type) — never your full URL or personal data.
  • Third-party services have their own privacy policies outside our control.

Cookies

We do not set non-essential cookies. Any cookies after clicking an affiliate link come from the third party.

Age gate

Affiliate redirect requires 18+ confirmation stored locally; not transmitted.
