🎪 Conventions & Events

Furry conventions are multi-day gatherings — usually held at a hotel or convention center — where furries meet, attend panels, buy art, dance, parade in fursuits, and raise money for charity. Below is a curated list of major upcoming and recent events, drawn from official con sites and furrycons.com. Always verify dates on the official convention site before booking; things occasionally shift.

🗓️ First time at a con?

Start with our first-con survival guide: what to pack, how to budget, badge etiquette, and why you should plan your sleep schedule. You don't need a fursuit to attend — most attendees don't wear one.

Finding smaller meets near you

Major conventions are only the visible tip. Most furries first meet the community at smaller events: local park meets, bowl-a-thons, BBQs, house parties, and regional bar-nights. The best places to find them:

  • furrycons.com — a maintained worldwide calendar including small regional meets.
  • Telegram and Discord regional groups — search for “furry [your city/region]” or ask on the major furry servers.
  • Your local con's Telegram/Discord — most cons run local meet channels year-round.
  • Reddit: r/furry's recurring meetup threads.

A note on safety & etiquette

Cons are generally well-run and welcoming, but they're also large gatherings. Standard advice for any event applies: read our safer-spaces guide, trust your gut, ask before hugging or touching fursuiters (pats are generally fine with permission, but grabbing or tackling is not), and remember that hotel staff and security are your allies if something goes wrong.
