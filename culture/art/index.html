Furry Art: Styles, Commissions, and Getting Started

Art is the engine of the fandom. Most furries are artists, commissioners, or enthusiastic appreciators.

Styles

Furry art spans chibi/cute, toony, painterly, hyperrealistic, pixel, anime-influenced, and more. Browse FurAffinity or Itaku to find what you like.

How to commission safely

  • Vibe-check the artist: finished work in their gallery, recent updates.
  • Read their TOS — refund policy, turnaround, commercial rights.
  • Pay via Goods & Services on PayPal; never use irreversible methods unless you know the artist.
  • Start small (headshot/sketch) before a full piece.
  • Keep receipts and communication logs.

Red flags

  • Demands full payment up front with no portfolio.
  • Public callouts for not delivering work; search "[name] beware" first.
  • Prices far below market rate — sometimes new, sometimes scam.

If you want to draw

Pick a species, study real anatomy, draw 100 bad heads, keep going. Tutorials on YouTube, free bases, feedback Discords exist. You don't need to be good to participate.
