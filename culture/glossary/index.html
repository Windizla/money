Furry Glossary

Furry jargon can sound like another language. Here are the terms you'll actually encounter.

  • Sona / Fursona — your anthropomorphic self-representation character.
  • Fursuit — costume of an anthropomorphic character.
  • Partial — head + paws + tail (no body suit).
  • Murrsuit — a fursuit used for adult purposes; kept separate from regular fursuiting by most.
  • Popufur — a popular/well-known fandom member.
  • Greymuzzle — an older, long-time fan (often 35+).
  • Con crud — the cold/flu you catch after a packed convention.
  • Dealer's Den — convention vendor hall.
  • Artist Alley — section for individual artists.
  • Badge — laminated art on a lanyard worn at cons.
  • Digi / Digitigrade — fursuit with animal-shaped padded legs.
  • Planti / Plantigrade — fursuit with human-shaped legs.
  • Ref sheet — reference drawing of your sona (front/back, colors, markings).
  • Base — pre-made line-art template for characters.
  • Commission — paying an artist to draw your character.
  • YCH — "Your Character Here": pre-composed scene the artist inserts your sona into.
  • Furpile / cuddle pile — group cuddle at a con; usually platonic.
  • Therian — someone identifying as non-physically being an animal; distinct from furry with some overlap.
  • Otherkin — identifying as non-human; broader than therianthropy.
