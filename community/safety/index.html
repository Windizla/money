Community Safety — Online and IRL

The fandom is overwhelmingly kind, but like any large community it has bad actors. Safety is a community practice.

Online

  • Protect your identity. If you need to keep furry life separate, use a pseudonym and separate accounts.
  • Block early and often. You owe nobody debate.
  • Don't send money to people you haven't met IRL.
  • Be careful with explicit photos. Assume anything digital could be shared.
  • Watch for grooming patterns: much older members focusing on very young ones, isolating from friends, pushing secrecy. Speak up to mods.

At meets and conventions

  • First meetings in public, daytime, around people.
  • Tell someone where you're going and who you're meeting.
  • Have your own transport and hotel key.
  • Don't accept open drinks; watch yours.
  • Consent is active and ongoing — hugs, photos, suit touches.
  • Know where con security is.

Consent culture

The fandom has developed unusually strong consent norms. "Don't touch a fursuiter without asking" is the most visible rule. Ask, listen, respect "no" as a full answer.
