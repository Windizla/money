Your First Furry Meetup or Convention

Meeting furries IRL for the first time is one of the best parts of the fandom — and can be nerve-wracking.

Start small: a local meet

Regional meets are usually low-key: coffee, park hang, BBQ, or a trip to a local event. Expect 5–50 people.

Preparing for a convention

  • Book hotels early — blocks sell out months ahead.
  • Budget for badge, hotel, transport, food, dealer's den, backup plan.
  • Pack: comfortable shoes, water bottle, snacks, hoodie, power bank, hand sanitizer.
  • Bring a badge with your fursona name and pronouns.
  • If fursuiting, bring a handler, water, cooling gear, repair kit.

Etiquette

  • Ask before hugging or touching anyone, especially in fursuit.
  • Ask before taking photos.
  • Hygiene matters — you'll be around people in costumes for days.
  • "Con crud" is real — sleep, hydrate, wash hands.

If you get overwhelmed

It's normal. Take breaks, leave the main space, return to your room. Nobody judges you for pacing yourself.
