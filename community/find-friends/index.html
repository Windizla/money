How to Find Friends in the Furry Fandom

Updated Aug 2026Reading time 8 min

The best way to make furry friends is simple to say and hard to do: show up somewhere, consistently, and be kind. The rest is details.

1. Pick one place and commit for 30 days

Joining ten Discord servers at once and never posting doesn't work. Pick one — a local meet, a con you plan to attend, an art server — and show up. Comment on art, post a sketch, ask questions. Consistency beats intensity.

2. Contribute, don't just consume

  • Comment thoughtfully on people's posts (not just "cute!").
  • Share references, organize game nights, help with setup at a local meet.
  • Ask curious, non-invasive questions about people's sonas.

3. If you're shy, start with text

Channels, art sites, and comment threads feel lower-pressure than voice chats and conventions.

4. Attend a local meet or a con

Offline friendships solidify faster. Local meets are small and casual; conventions have overwhelming density of shared context.

5. Be patient

Most fandom friendships take months to gel. Keep showing up.

⚠️ Safety

Not everyone in the fandom is a good friend. Trust your instincts and read community safety.
