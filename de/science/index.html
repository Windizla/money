🔬 Furscience

A sober, evidence-based look at the furry fandom, built on peer-reviewed research from the International Anthropomorphic Research Project (IARP, a.k.a. Furscience) and other published sources. This section is not monetized — it exists to be cited, to correct myths, and to help the world understand who furries actually are.

📌 Editorial policy

This is a trust zone. No affiliate links appear anywhere inside /science/. Where a statistic is not yet confirmed by a primary source, it is marked [pending primary source verification] and will be verified before publication. Claims are sourced; opinions are labeled as such.
