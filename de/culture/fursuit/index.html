Fursuits: Types, Cost, Care, and Makers

Fursuits are costumes of anthropomorphic animal characters — one of the most visible parts of the fandom, and one that newcomers are often curious about.

Types

  • Partial — head, paws, tail, sometimes feet; worn with regular clothes. Cheapest and coolest.
  • Plantigrade fullsuit — covers the body with human-shaped legs.
  • Digitigrade fullsuit — padded legs for animal shape; more expensive, hotter.
  • Quadsuit — wearer on all fours with extended legs; rare, specialized.

Cost (2026 ranges)

  • Used head: $200–$800
  • New partial from emerging maker: $800–$1500
  • Fullsuit mid-tier: $1800–$3500
  • Top-tier fullsuit: $3500–$8000+

Expect 6–24 month waits for commissions. How to choose a maker.

Care

Balaclava and under-armor are mandatory. Disinfect the head after every wear, brush the fur, hang to dry. Never machine-wash a head.
