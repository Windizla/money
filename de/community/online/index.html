Where Furries Hang Out Online

There's no single "furry Facebook." Furries use several major platforms for different needs.

The big ones

  • Discord — The default. General, regional, art, species, LGBTQ+ servers. Look for active mods and clear rules.
  • Telegram — Huge in Eastern Europe and Asia; regional groups are often here.
  • Art sites — FurAffinity (largest), Itaku (growing), Weasyl/SoFurry (quieter).
  • Twitter/X — Artists, less of a home than it used to be.
  • Twitch — Furry streamers and art streams.

For meeting people
