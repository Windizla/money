Affiliate Disclosure

Furlore is reader-supported. Some pages contain affiliate links to partner services. This page explains exactly how that works.

What an affiliate link is

An affiliate link is a tracked link. If you click and sign up, we earn a commission from the service at no extra cost to you.

How we select partner services

  • Active, well-moderated, LGBTQ+ friendly.
  • 18+ only for dating.
  • No services with known patterns of predatory billing, fake profiles, or abuse.

Editorial independence

  • We never write positive pieces because a service pays us.
  • We will criticize partner services when they deserve it.
  • The science section carries zero affiliate links.
  • Every affiliate link has a disclosure note next to it (FTC compliance).

Why this model

Running a good reference site isn't free. Affiliate revenue lets us stay free for readers, run no ads, and use no tracking — while paying contributors fairly.
