About Furlore

Furlore is an independent, community-focused site about the furry fandom. We provide honest information for new furries, curious outsiders (parents, partners, journalists), and existing members looking for well-written guides.

What makes us different

  • Honest about monetization. Affiliate links are clearly labeled. No pop-ups, sticky banners, or deceptive CTAs. See disclosure.
  • The science section is not monetized. We wall off Furscience content from affiliate links to preserve credibility.
  • No made-up numbers. Unverified statistics are tagged {{FACT}} until confirmed.
  • No tracking. We run no analytics. Sub-IDs on affiliate links tell us which pages are useful but nothing about individuals.
